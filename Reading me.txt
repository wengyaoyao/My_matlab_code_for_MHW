Explanation of the Attached Files

The <figure> folder includes both my results and the corresponding figures from your paper for comparison.

Map 1 shows the sea surface temperature anomaly. 
We selected the same three-month period (indicated in the figure title) as in Figures 3h–q of the article to verify the correctness of the downloaded data. 
The spatial patterns are generally consistent with the published results, suggesting that the input data are correctly obtained and processed.

Map 2 presents the marine heatwave (MHW) duration (in days).
When compared with Figure 4e in the paper, the maximum duration near the Java Island in my result appears lower than that reported in the article.

Map 3 shows the maximum MHW intensity. Similar to Map 2, the peak intensity in my figure is weaker than that shown in Figure 4f, particularly in the northwestern Australian region.
